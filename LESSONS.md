# Lessons for KOML

The domain needs explicit expectations before health can be checked: a due date, budget, progress and blocker state. Risk signals are different from predictions. A completed project can still exceed budget, and overlapping reasons require unique flagged-project counts.

Validated assumption: pure rules can match independently planted ground truth exactly on realistic synthetic combinations, including boundaries. Unvalidated assumption: these three rules are sufficient for Henry's real operational decisions or that a buyer will pay. No real-use evidence was collected during this build.

Operating rule for future builds: calculate fixture expectations independently of the engine, mutate the inputs to prove results are computed, and record adoption and error evidence before choosing a commercial verdict.
